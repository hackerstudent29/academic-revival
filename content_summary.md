# MSAJCE Website Content Analysis

I have analyzed the provided `college datas` directory, which contains raw scraped text and tables from the previous college website across 5 batch documents. 

Here is a summary of the extracted data architecture, which serves as the content foundation for the new website.

## 1. Admissions & Academics (Batch 1)
- **Courses Offered**: Details all Undergraduate (B.E./B.Tech) and Postgraduate (M.E.) programs including their intake capacities (e.g., CSE: 120, IT: 60, AI&DS: 60, newly added Cyber Security & AIML).
- **Admissions**: Eligibility criteria for regular and lateral entry, plus TANCET/GATE requirements for PG.
- **Academics**: Curriculums and Syllabi structures mapped to Anna University Regulations 2021 & 2024.
- **Financial Aid**: Details on Government Scholarships (SC/ST, First Graduate) and internal Mohamed Sathak Trust Merit Scholarships.

## 2. Core & Overview (Batch 2)
- **Institutional Identity**: The official Vision & Mission statements.
- **Leadership Profiles**: Messages and profiles of the Chairman, Director, and Principal.
- **History**: Background of the Mohamed Sathak Trust and the establishment of MSAJCE.

## 3. Research, Exams & Campus Facilities (Batch 3)
- **Facilities**: Information on Library resources, Hostel life, Sports complexes, Auditorium, and Transport fleet.
- **Research**: R&D cell initiatives, patents, publications, and funding grants.
- **Examinations**: Exam cell procedures, rules, and Anna University COE integration.

## 4. Accreditation, Compliance & Student Portal (Batch 4)
- **NAAC Documentation**: Detailed breakdown of the 7 NAAC criteria, metric IDs, and weightages.
- **Best Practices**: Highlights the "Technology Centres" and "Afternoon Laboratory Classes" initiatives.
- **Code of Conduct**: Strict rules regarding 75% attendance, dress code, mobile phone usage, and faculty expectations.
- **IQAC**: Internal Quality Assurance Cell objectives, functions, and full committee rosters for previous academic years.
- **Rankings**: Details on NIRF (Engineering, Overall, Innovation) participation.
- **Student Portal**: Academic schedules, circulars, and lists of Department Toppers.

## 5. Technology Centres & Flagship Initiatives (Batch 5)
- **The 19 Technology Centres**: Deep dive into the specialized experiential learning hubs designed to make students industry-ready (e.g., CISCO networking, Altair simulation, Automation Anywhere). 
- **Certifications**: Industry-recognized courses offered beyond the core Anna University curriculum.

---

### How to use this data:
This extracted content is incredibly valuable! Whenever we build out new pages (like the *Admissions*, *Academics*, or *Facilities* pages), we have all the correct intake numbers, exact names of the 19 tech centers, code of conduct rules, and NAAC summaries ready to be dropped into our modern UI components.
