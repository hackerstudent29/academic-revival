## 📌 1. An Overview (`about.php`)

### About the Institution
The **Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE)**, established on **5th July 2001** under the aegis of **Mohamed Sathak Trust**, is approved by **AICTE New Delhi**, affiliated to **Anna University Chennai** and Government of Tamil Nadu. 

MSAJCE strives to continuously upgrade its facilities to provide quality technical education to meet industrial and societal needs by providing skill-based training with state-of-the-art facilities.

The college campus, sprawling over **70 acres of greenery**, is located inside the **SIPCOT IT Park, Siruseri**, surrounded by multinational IT companies such as TCS, CTS, Intellect, Aspire, Steria, Polaris, FSS, etc.

MSAJCE has excellent infrastructure facilities including spacious classrooms, drawing halls, laboratories, seminar halls, computer facilities with internet & Wi-Fi connectivity, library, workshops, auditorium, and sports grounds.

A team of well-qualified and experienced faculty supports the teaching and learning process with equal importance given to academic, co-curricular, and extracurricular activities.

### Why Join MSAJCE?
- **Prime IT Hub Location:** Sprawling 70-acre green campus located in Siruseri IT Park surrounded by 100+ leading IT industries (TCS, CTS, Infosys, Intellect, FSS, HCL, etc.).
- **State-of-the-Art Infrastructure:** Industry-collaborative technology centers and modern labs.

## 📌 3. Management & Trust History (`ourhistory.php`)

### Mohamed Sathak Trust History
**Mohamed Sathak Trust**, a charitable and educational organization, was established on **26 October 1973** at Kilakarai by the philanthropic Mohamed Sathak Family of Kilakarai, Ramanathapuram District, Tamil Nadu. The primary objective is helping deprived people from rural and financially challenged backgrounds to obtain quality education.

The first self-financing engineering college in Tamil Nadu (*Mohamed Sathak Engineering College*) was established by the Trust in 1984 at Kilakarai. Today, the Trust manages **18 educational institutions** across Kilakarai, Ramanathapuram, and Chennai spanning Engineering, Technology, Arts & Science, Architecture, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Nursing, and Schooling.
