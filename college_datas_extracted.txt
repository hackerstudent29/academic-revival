National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC)
Overview
India has one of the largest and diverse education systems in the world. Privatization, widespread expansion, increased autonomy and introduction of Programmes in new and emerging areas have improved access to higher education. At the same time, it has also led to widespread concern on the quality and relevance of higher education. To address these concerns, the National Policy on Education (NPE, 1986) and the Programme of Action (PoA, 1992) spelt out strategic plans for policies, advocating the establishment of an independent National accreditation agency. Consequently, the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) was established in 1994 as an autonomous institution of the University Grants Commission (UGC) with its Headquarters in Bengaluru. The mandate of NAAC as reflected in its vision statement is in making quality assurance an integral part of the functioning of Higher Education Institutions (HEIs).

Best Practices
Best Practice 1: Technology Centres
Title: Technology Centres for Multi-Disciplinary Skills and Industry Certifications
Objectives: To provide hands-on training through experiential learning and enable students to obtain certifications in cutting-edge technologies from reputable training agencies.
Context: To ensure students are deployable, they are trained in multi-disciplinary skills beyond their core domain through 19 specialized technology centres within regular college hours.
Practice: Split scheduling: Forenoon for regular academics, Afternoon dedicated to training, projects, and lab practice. Trainers are certified internal faculty. Centres remain accessible beyond college working hours.
Evidence of Success: Student certifications by CISCO, Altair, Automation Anywhere; two courses recognized by Anna University as audit courses.
Best Practice 2: Afternoon Laboratory Classes
Title: Scheduling Practical Courses in Afternoon Sessions for Experiential Learning
Objectives: Enhance problem-solving and critical thinking skills without inducing fatigue between theoretical lectures.
Practice: All lab courses are scheduled in the afternoon; industry experts are invited during afternoon slots to demonstrate industrial practices.
Code of Conduct
A. Code of Conduct for Students
Mandatory adherence to rules and discipline; zero tolerance for discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, or language.
Minimum 75% attendance per semester as per Anna University regulations.
Proper dress code, formal shoes, and visible display of student ID cards.
Mobile phones and laptops permitted on campus but prohibited during lecture hours and examinations.
Responsible social media usage with strict prohibition against defamatory content.
B. Code of Conduct for Teaching Faculty
Punctuality to duty and classes; monitor student dress code and grooming.
Continuous verification of student notebooks (at least 5 students daily per class).
Timely completion of syllabus as per lesson plan approved by HoDs.
Active involvement in research, publishing minimum 2 papers annually in SCI / Anna University Annexure 1 / UGC Care journals.
C. Code of Conduct for Non-Teaching & Lab Staff
Maintain laboratory log books, ensure equipment safety, guide students during practical tasks, and conduct periodic maintenance and calibration.
Sole responsibility for opening, closing, and securing lab machinery and instruments.
D. Code of Conduct for Administrative & Support Staff
Punctual adherence to office timings, maintenance of floor cleanliness, rapid rectification of electrical/plumbing issues, and ethical communication.

Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)
Objectives
To develop a system for conscious, consistent, and catalytic action to improve the academic and administrative performance of the institution.
To promote measures for institutional functioning towards quality enhancement through internalization of quality culture and institutionalization of best practices.
Functions of IQAC
Development and application of quality benchmarks for academic and administrative activities.
Facilitating the creation of a learner-centric environment and faculty development.
Collection and analysis of feedback from students, parents, alumni, and employers.
Preparation of the Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR) for submission to NAAC.

Technology Centre
In pursuit of MSAJCE vision and mission to make the students deployable and equip them with the upcoming emerging industrial demands, various Technology Centres to educate, train and up-skill students in niche technologies established in the year 2019-2020. The courses and training are designed based on the recommendations of AICTE and India Skill Report 2020. The Technology Centers established with state of art facility, provide an ambient learning environment for research and innovation. Each centre is collaborated with industry / government organizations to provide certifications, onsite / offsite projects and internships. The list of industries include Automation Anywhere, Godrej Inc, Ford, Palo Alto systems, Amazon Web Services, National Instruments, CISCO Academy, Altair, Open wave computing, Levergent Technologies, Propeller Technologies etc.

Department of Civil Engineering
Overview
Civil engineers conceive, design, construct, supervise, operate and maintain infrastructure projects and system in the public and private sector including roads, building, bridges, airports, tunnels, dams, systems for water supply and sewerage treatment, pollution controls etc. Civil Engineering is one of the older engineering disciplines and it is a backbone and integral part of the society, without them a society could not function. Civil Engineering is a very diverse profession and offers a wide range of activities through various disciplines such as surveying, structural engineering, transport engineering, environment and water supply engineering, Remote Sensing and GIS, etc... Civil engineers are the heroes of the engineering world and part of helping society by adapting the infrastructures with new technologies, to meet the population growth and climatic change. Civil engineers will never have a dull moment and always creative in their own unique way.
Welcome to the Department of Civil Engineering, Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering, Siruseri, Chennai, India. The Department of Civil Engineering is one of the pioneer departments of the Institute, established in 2007. The department has excelled in the areas of Academics, Research, Cultural and Sports activities developing a prestige up to global standards.
The department currently offers one Undergraduate program, namely B.E Civil Engineering and Postgraduate program, namely M.E Structural Engineering. The framework of the program provides ample opportunities for the students to get trained as an Industrialist, Researchers or Entrepreneur. The department has produced eminent engineers who are committed to the highest level of professional standards in the filed of Engineering and Technology.

Overview
The role of Information Technology is vital in every aspect of human life. The information technology is the study and use of system for storing, retrieving and processing information. With the introduction of computers, the business world has changed forever. Information technology helps the business world to view changes in the global markets faster than they usually do. Due to the development of this technology, globalization has increased and the world is brought closer. Information is stored and shared quickly and easily across the globe. The key field includes Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Networking, Mobile computing, Block Chain and so on have tremendous job opening.
Programme Details

Department of Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design & Technology)
Overview
B.Tech in Electronics Engineering with Specialization in VLSI Design & Technology. The proliferation of digital economy and consumer electronics has increased the demand for integrated chips and sensors exponentially. Meeting this burgeoning domestic demand is very challenging. Currently, India is importing integrated chips in billions of US dollar. Post-COVID global semiconductor market has grown exponentially with $590 billion in 2021 and also expected to grow to $1 Trillion by 2030. The driving force of this tremendous growth is the technologies such as Artificial Intelligence(AI), Electric Vehicle (EV), 5G Networks, Internet of Things (IoT), Health Care etc.
Realization of these technologies depends on the production of silicon chips for hardware. Many chip-making industries in R&D, design, manufacturing, testing, etc. are established to fill the gap in the chip supply chain. Overall semiconductor import has surpassed that of gold, standing second only to crude oil.
Programme Details
B.E. - Electronics Engg. (VLSI Design & Technology): 4 years, 8 Semesters, Intake: 30.
Industry Drivers & Forecast
Semiconductor Industry Growth (PLI scheme, India Semiconductor Mission)
5G and 6G Technologies (RFIC, mixed-signal design, high-speed digital circuit design)
Automotive Electronics (ADAS, EV power management, sensors)
IoT and Consumer Electronics (Low-power SoC design)
AI & ML Hardware (AI accelerators, GPUs, FPGAs)
Vision
To be a premier centre for semiconductor and chip design education, preparing highly proficient VLSI design and fabrication engineers.

An Overview
Mohamed Sathak A. J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE) was established in 2001 by the Mohamed Sathak Trust with the noble vision of imparting quality technical education to students across diverse socio-economic backgrounds. The college is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.
Located in the IT Corridor along Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR) inside SIPCOT IT Park, Siruseri, Chennai, the campus provides state-of-the-art infrastructure, well-equipped laboratories, modern digital libraries, and dedicated innovation centres.
Objectives
To deliver quality engineering education through experiential learning.
To bridge the gap between academia and industry through cutting-edge technology centres.
To foster research, innovation, and entrepreneurship among students and faculty members.
To instill ethical values, leadership qualities, and social commitment.

The Mohamed Sathak Trust was established on 26th October 1973 by the Philanthropic Mohamed Sathak Family of Kilakarai, Ramanathapuram District, Tamil Nadu. Research and Development
Objectives
The Research and Development Cell aims to nurture research culture in the College by promoting research in newly emerging and challenging areas of Engineering, Technology, Science and Humanities:
To encourage the students and faculty members to carry out research in the fields of Engineering, Technology, Science and Humanities by providing necessary facilities and infrastructures required for carrying out research.
To create zeal among the students and faculty towards research and innovation and to encourage them to publish their findings in conferences and journals.
To assist the students to apply funding for conducting research under student project scheme to various funding agencies like TNSCST, MSME, IEI (I) etc.
To work closely with the industry to understand their needs that eventually will result in new or improved products, processes, systems or services that can increase the company's productivity and also promote Institute-Industry Collaboration.
To support the government in serving the society by taking up sponsored projects offered by agencies such as TNSCST, DST, AICTE, UGC, ISRO, DRDO, CSIR, DBT etc.
To enhance the general research capability of budding engineers by way of participating in conferences, seminars, workshops, project competition, etc.
To organize various workshops/ seminar/ trainings related to promotion of research.
To encourage both students and faculty to apply copyrights and patents.

Hostel Facilities
Overview
A home away from home is provided separately for both boys and girls, accommodating 480 boy students and 210 girl students.
Boys Hostel: Located inside the college campus. It consists of 3 blocks with 233 Non-AC rooms and 6 AC rooms, accommodating 2 students per room. Equipped with TV hall, Wi-Fi, indoor games, and study facilities open until 7:00 PM.
Girls Hostel: Located at Sholinganallur (5 km from campus) in a safe and secure residential zone. Consists of 1 block with 71 rooms accommodating 3 students per room, with attached bath/toilet, entertainment hall, Wi-Fi, and library/computer access until 9:00 PM.

Social Services — NSS, YRC & Unnat Bharat Abhiyan
National Service Scheme (NSS)
Established at MSAJCE in 2001 with over 100 active student volunteers from 1st and 2nd years.
Motto & Goal: "Serve the downtrodden in the society" and "Personality Development through Community Service".
Volunteers Code: Work under nominated group leaders, avoid controversial issues, record daily activities, wear NSS badge on duty.
Youth Red Cross (YRC)
Established at MSAJCE in 2014 under Indian Red Cross Society principles (Humanity, Impartiality, Neutrality, Voluntary Service). Offers First Aid training and community health awareness.
Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA)
Inspired by Gandhian philosophy of self-sufficient village republics, connecting higher education institutions with rural communities for participatory technology solutions.

SECTION 1: Overview of Technology Centres
Source URL: https://msajce-edu.in/technologycentre.phpStatus: OK
In pursuit of MSAJCE vision and mission to make the students deployable and equip them with upcoming emerging industrial demands, various Technology Centres to educate, train and up-skill students in niche technologies were established in the year 2019-2020. The courses and training are designed based on the recommendations of AICTE and India Skill Report 2020. The Technology Centers established with state-of-the-art facilities provide an ambient learning environment for research and innovation. Each centre collaborates with industry / government organizations to provide certifications, onsite / offsite projects and internships. Collaborating industries include Automation Anywhere, Godrej Inc, Ford, Palo Alto Networks, Amazon Web Services (AWS), National Instruments, CISCO Academy, Altair, Openwave Computing, Levergent Technologies, Propeller Technologies, etc.
SECTION 2: Mechanical & Civil Engineering Technology Centres
1. Centre for 3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing
Domain: Mechanical Engineering & Product Prototyping
Industry Partners: Altair, MakerBot, Propeller Technologies
Facilities & Equipment: FDM 3D Printers, SLA Prototyping systems, slicing engines (Cura, Simplify3D), 3D scanning hardware.
Key Modules: Rapid Prototyping, CAD-to-Print conversion, Generative Design, Reverse Engineering, Material Extrusion testing.
Certification & Projects: Additive Manufacturing Technician Certification, student design prototypes for hackathons and patents.
2. Centre for Computer Aided Engineering (CAE)
Source PDF: 02CAE.pdf
Domain: Mechanical, Structural & Finite Element Analysis
Industry Collaboration: Altair Engineering (HyperWorks), ANSYS
Facilities & Software: Altair HyperMesh, OptiStruct, RADIOSS, ANSYS Mechanical, SolidWorks Simulation.
Key Modules: Linear & Non-Linear Static Analysis, Dynamic Simulation, Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), Structural Optimization, Crash/Impact Analysis.
3. Centre for Industrial Robotics
Domain: Mechanical, Mechatronics & Automation
Industry Partners: FANUC, Yaskawa, Levergent Technologies
Facilities & Equipment: 6-Axis Articulated Industrial Robot Arm, SCARA Pick-and-Place robot, Teach Pendants, PLC-interfaced controllers.
Key Modules: Robot Kinematics, Trajectory Planning, End-Effector Design, Robot Programming, Robotic Welding/Painting simulations.
4. Centre for Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)
Domain: Civil & Mechanical Material Quality Assurance
Certification Standards: ASNT / ISNT Level-II certification alignment
Facilities & Testing Methods: Ultrasonic Flaw Detectors, Magnetic Particle Testing (MPT), Liquid Penetrant Testing (LPT), Radiographic Film Interpretation, Eddy Current Testing.

5. Centre for Building Information Modelling (BIM)

Software Suite: Autodesk Revit Architecture, Revit Structure, Navisworks, BIM 360

Key Modules: 3D Architectural Modelling, 4D Construction Scheduling, 5D Cost Estimation, Clash Detection, Structural Reinforcement Detailing.

Overview
Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat is an initiative launched by the Government of India to enhance unity in diversity. EBSB was launched by Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi during the Rashtriya Ekta Diwas on 31st October, 2015, to commemorate the birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. The programme aims to establish structured cultural connections in language learning, culture, traditions, music, tourism, cuisine, and sports through state pairing.
State Pairing: Tamil Nadu is paired with Jammu-Kashmir and Ladakh.
Partner Institution: Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering conducted collaborative cultural exchange programs with SSM College of Engineering, Parihaspora, Pattan, Baramulla, Jammu & Kashmir.

PAGE: Kaushal Augmentation and Restructuring Mission of AICTE (KARMA)
Source URL: https://msajce-edu.in/karma.phpStatus: OK
Overview & Objectives
AICTE initiated the KARMA scheme to overcome the dual challenge of scarcity of skilled manpower in jobs and low skill levels of those in employment. It utilizes higher education infrastructure during off-hours for skill training as per National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) aligned standards.
The Three KARMA Skilling Models
Model 1: Targeting school dropouts after class 10th pass.
Model 2: Targeting advanced level skilling of students already studying in AICTE approved technical institutions (offered during 4th to 7th semesters).
Model 3: Integrating school education with higher education via a Hub-and-Spoke model.
Approved Courses at MSAJCE
