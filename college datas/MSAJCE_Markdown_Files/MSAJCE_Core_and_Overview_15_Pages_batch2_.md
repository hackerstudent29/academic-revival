## An Overview

Mohamed Sathak A. J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE) was established in 2001 by the Mohamed Sathak Trust with the noble vision of imparting quality technical education to students across diverse socio-economic backgrounds. The college is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.

Located in the IT Corridor along Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR) inside SIPCOT IT Park, Siruseri, Chennai, the campus provides state-of-the-art infrastructure, well-equipped laboratories, modern digital libraries, and dedicated innovation centres.

### Objectives

- To deliver quality engineering education through experiential learning.

- To bridge the gap between academia and industry through cutting-edge technology centres.

- To foster research, innovation, and entrepreneurship among students and faculty members.

- To instill ethical values, leadership qualities, and social commitment.

## Mohamed Sathak Trust

The Mohamed Sathak Trust was established on 26th October 1973 by the Philanthropic Mohamed Sathak Family of Kilakarai, Ramanathapuram District, Tamil Nadu. The Trust was founded with the primary objective of promoting higher education, technical proficiency, and technical research among minority and underprivileged communities.

## Anti-Ragging Committee & Squad

Ragging is strictly prohibited in all areas inside and outside the college campus, hostels, buses, and canteens. Any student found guilty of ragging will be dealt with severely in accordance with the UGC/AICTE regulations and the Tamil Nadu Prohibition of Ragging Act.

### Anti-Ragging Measures

- Display of anti-ragging warning boards across campus, canteens, and hostels.

- Continuous vigil maintained by the Anti-Ragging Squad during class hours, breaks, and in college transport.

- Dedicated 24/7 helpline and student grievance dropboxes.

## Functional Committees

To facilitate smooth, participative, and decentralized governance, the institution operates through structured functional committees:

1.  **Library Committee:** Guides book acquisition, journal subscriptions, and digital repository maintenance.

2.  **Transport Committee:** Manages bus routes, fleet safety, and student transport passes.

3.  **Canteen & Mess Committee:** Monitors food quality, hygiene standards, and dietary pricing.

4.  **Sports & Cultural Committee:** Plans annual athletic meets, inter-collegiate tournaments, and cultural fests.

5.  **Discipline & Ethics Committee:** Enforces student dress code, attendance, and on-campus conduct.

6.  **Career Guidance & Placement Committee:** Coordinates campus recruitment drives, soft-skill training, and aptitude programmes.

7.  **Research & Innovation Advisory Committee (RAC):** Promotes funded research projects, patent filings, and IPR activities.

8.  **Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC):** Formulates benchmarks and evaluates institutional quality parameters.

- **E-Governance Policy:** Automation of admissions, fee collection, examinations, library operations, and student records.
